Job Readiness levels explained

Job Readiness is IU's framework for measuring how well a course prepares students for AI-era professional practice.

Level 0 — Domain Mastery: Learning objectives focused on foundational knowledge and skills within the discipline. No job-readiness or AI integration.
Level 1 — Domain + Professional Mode or AI Fluency: Adds either one professional mode or basic AI competencies (prompting, verifying outputs, understanding limitations).
Level 2 — Domain + Mode + AI: Adds learning objectives for using AI to strengthen a specific professional mode in practice.
Level 3 — Domain + Multiple Modes + AI: Adds meta-cognitive skills for switching between professional modes and calibrating AI use for each context.
Level 4 — Full Integration: Students fluidly integrate domain expertise, mode-switching, and AI competencies with professional judgment.

Data as of Jun 2026. Per-course level data will be tracked individually in future reporting cycles.

Scope & methodology

Covers CoCoHub course book script XML and KFK exam question sets (Dec 2025 – Jun 2026), plus Job Readiness PLF project courses reported as a separate stream above. Does not include video updates. Course book changes classified by AI analysis of XML diffs between the two most recent published versions per course; only changes with visible text differences are counted. KFK question edits reflect courses where at least one question was revised between published versions — questions are edited by creating a revised version alongside the original; the count reflects old/new pairs with visible content differences. Courses with only structural XML changes and no visible text difference are excluded from the exam question count. Two courses (DLBMETDSM01_E, DLMAIAI01-01) are included in the course count but excluded from confirmed edit totals and examples due to parse errors in their source XML. Magnitude buckets: minor 1–2 edits, moderate 3–8, major 9+.
