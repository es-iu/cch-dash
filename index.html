Method. Each course's latest published version is read in full (capped at 120k characters) and scored by an LLM against a fixed six-dimension rubric; weights: practical relevance .25, factual correctness .20, and .15 each for up-to-dateness, job readiness and exam alignment, .10 clarity. Overall = weighted mean ÷ 5 × 100. The predecessor (before) score is re-judged from the authored changes, so Δ reflects genuine editorial work. Scores are heuristic and should be read within discipline: pure-humanities books have a lower natural ceiling on practical relevance and job readiness and should not be compared head-to-head with applied IT/business courses.
